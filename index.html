Beaumont Tower - marks the site of College Hall, the first building on the Michigan State University campus. College Hall wasn't built properly and was eventually demolished after two walls collapsed. The tower was named in honor of John Beaumont, a significant benefactor of the university. This information can be found at Michigan State University libraries.
